Arena

A first-person prototype with fast movement. Every model, texture and sound is generated in code, so you can swap in your own assets later.

Touch

Left thumb moves, push to the edge to sprint. Right thumb aims. Buttons: Fire, Jump, Slide, Reload.

Keyboard and mouse

WASD, mouse, Shift sprint, Space jump, C slide or crouch, E plant, R reload. Hold the right mouse button to look if the cursor does not lock.

Controller

Sticks move and aim, A jump, B slide, X reload, Y plant, R2 fire.

Slide: sprint, then press Slide. Slide cancel: press Jump or Slide again mid-slide to keep your speed. Bunny hop: press Jump again within a split second of landing to keep and build speed. Late presses lose it.
